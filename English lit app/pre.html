Context

Wordsworth was a romantic poet. He spent a lot of time outdoors to flee from his troubled home life, growing up to see nature as sublime and appreciating its intimidating aspects.

Form and structure

Frequent enjambment is used, showing how the speaker cannot properly express their thoughts as they are overwhelmed and scarred.
The epic poem evokes sympathy from the reader as they are also ovewhelmed by the immensity of nature's power.
The use of blank verse may perhaps show the uniformity of nature and create an overwhelming sense of besiegement.
