Summary

John Agard criticises the way history was taught to him and celebrates the people and cultures that were excluded from his education. The poem explores themes of identity, power, and the importance of understanding one's heritage.

Context

Checking Out Me History was written by John Agard, a poet from Guyana who later moved to Britain. The poem reflects his experiences of learning a British-focused history while being taught very little about Black historical figures and Caribbean culture. Agard challenges this imbalance and argues that knowledge of one's own history is essential for forming a strong sense of identity.
